            Method

            History from Q1 2023 to Q4 2026 comes from dated public data; the evidence ledger lists every starting value with its status (reported, survey, estimate, forecast, index or assumption) and its source. From 2027 a quarterly system-dynamics model takes over. Organisations adopt AI along an S-curve (potential work); cheaper tasks raise the work wanted; compute is bought, powered and allocated with paid work first, so realised work can fall short of what is wanted. AI revenue is the value of realised work times the share vendors capture. Capex responds to sentiment, utilisation, power and investor patience; prices follow hardware, efficiency, energy, scarcity and competition; credit stress follows leverage, how much of the capital charge on five years of capex revenue covers, drawdowns and idle chips. Each scenario moves fourteen drivers, and the behavioural equations are calibrated so the first simulated quarter continues the Q4 2026 values.

            Hype, credit, bank, lab-distress and signal-to-noise are illustrative indices, not probabilities. Sector series are stated mappings of economy-wide outputs. Treat every path as a coherent story, not a forecast. Nothing here is investment advice.

              How to use the monitor

              1. Pick a scenario in the top bar (keys 1–8). Every chart switches; faint lines show the other scenarios for comparison.
              2. Move through time: drag the timeline above the charts, click any chart, or use ← →. The big date and all numbers follow the cursor.
              3. Press Play (Space) to animate 2023 → 2035. Tour plays all eight scenarios in a row.
              4. Tilt the drivers or switch on shocks on the left to build your own run. It appears as CUSTOM (dashed line, key 9).
              5. Sector lens (below the charts) translates each scenario for AECO firms, AECO software and software development.
              6. Broadcast (B) opens a 16:9 full-screen view made for presenting or recording. Where saving files is allowed, the red Record button saves a video file.
              7. Hover any chart, node or heatmap cell for the full numbers and the reasons behind them.
              8. Check any number: click a KPI tile, a lane value, a ledger row, a signpost or a heatmap cell to see its source (history) or its formula (model). Every panel has an ⓘ explainer.
              9. Guide (?) opens the 60-second tour, explain mode and Sources & methods — every source, formula and the verification process.

              This is a scenario model, not a forecast. History is dated public data to 17 October 2026; everything after is the model. Version 3 was revised after independent reviews by Gemini, DeepSeek, Grok and ChatGPT; see the evidence ledger and the review brief for what changed.
